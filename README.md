# Universal Design for Learning (UDL) – Moved to Docsify-This

> The Pressbooks book [Universal Design for Learning (UDL) for Inclusion, Diversity, Equity, and Accessibility (IDEA) 2nd Edition](https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/universaldesign2/), brought into Grav Open Publishing Space by the [Pressbooks Converter](https://pressbooks-converter.hibbittsdesign.org), then moved to [Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/#/) as plain Markdown pages by the [Open Publishing Course Space Bridge](https://open-publishing-course-space-bridge.hibbittsdesign.org).

These pages are the bridge's output as it is, shown like the book in Open Publishing Space: the home page is the book's home page, starting with its cover, title, author and licence, then a card for each module. The sidebar starts with the book's name (a link back to its home page) and lists its modules and pages, and Previous/Next buttons follow the pages in order.

The original book is by Darla Benton Kearney, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Its Grav version is at https://demo.hibbittsdesign.org/variants/grav-open-publishing-quark2-pressbooks-udl – this version uses the same content:

- `home.md` – the book's home page, with its cover image beside it
- `section-1/` – Front Matter: about this guide, land acknowledgement, accessibility statement, preface and contributors
- `section-2/` … `section-7/` – Modules 1 to 6, each with its pages
- `section-8/` – Glossary

Only the book is moved – the Grav site's other pages, such as its blog and Read Me, stay there. Images stay on Pressbooks, as in the Grav version.

The pages are clean Markdown, without the bridge's hidden notes. The bridge can still turn them into an Open Publishing Space book, like any Docsify site, but not exactly.
